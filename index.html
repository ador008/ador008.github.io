About me

Technology and competition, one mindset

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering graduate, now doing an MSc in Cybersecurity at Aalborg University Copenhagen. Alongside my studies, I have spent years in competitive esports as a player, team captain, shoutcaster and tournament organizer.

I have played Mobile Legends: Bang Bang since 2017, with teams like Red Viperz, Venom Esports and KINGSMEN, which I led as team leader. In 2023 I captained Team Bangladesh at the IESF Asia Qualifier in Saudi Arabia.

Today I live in Denmark, where I'm building a home for mobile esports through Mobile Esports Denmark.
